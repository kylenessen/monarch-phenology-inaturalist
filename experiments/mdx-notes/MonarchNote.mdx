Human-labeled larvae and chrysalises across North America. The map loops through the weeks of a year, pooling all years. Pause or drag the timeline, zoom, and change Hexagon size to adjust H3 resolution. Darker orange means more observations.

## Temperature before observation

Each observation contributes its coldest daily minimum and hottest daily maximum from the seven days before it was recorded. Bars count observations. These histograms pool all years and weeks and do not follow the map's animation. Use their controls to change life stage, units or bins.
