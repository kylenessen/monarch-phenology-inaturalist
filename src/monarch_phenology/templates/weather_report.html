What this can tell us

The dashed temperature lines mark freezing and your proposed 100°F heat threshold. They are reference lines, not fitted biological limits.

These are conditions associated with observations. An empty bin does not establish that breeding cannot occur there. Observer effort, season, latitude, host plants, and repeated visits can all shape these distributions.

AET is a landscape water flux, not humidity or migration itself. Its missing values are especially common in urban pixels, so the AET subset can differ from the temperature sample.

Methods, sampling, and sources

The California coastal group is inside California and within 50 km of the Natural Earth coastline, measured in California Albers coordinates. Eastern North America is east of 100°W. The third group contains the remaining western observations. These are analysis groups, not population boundaries. Seasons are December–February, March–May, June–August, and September–November.

Daymet V4 R1 estimates daily two-meter air temperature by interpolating weather-station observations to a 1 km grid. These are gridded estimates, not a thermometer at each monarch. We use the minimum of the 14 daily minima and the maximum of the 14 daily maxima, excluding the observation day. All 14 dates must be available. Daymet omits December 31 in leap years, so affected windows are excluded rather than silently filled.

MODIS MOD16A2GF V061 provides modeled actual evapotranspiration on a nominal 500 m grid in eight-day composites. We sum ET over the last two fully completed periods before the observation, then divide by their total days. This is normally a 16-day window ending one to eight days before the observation. The year-end period is shorter. It is not a daily AET median or an exact 14-day estimate. No composite includes the observation day or later days, although this gap-filled product is processed retrospectively using annual inputs.

MODIS ET values are multiplied by 0.1 to obtain millimeters per composite. Both composites must have valid, nonnegative ET, a good MODLAND flag, and a science quality flag of 0 or 1. Urban, water, sparse vegetation, other fill values, and rejected quality flags remain missing. We use the containing pixel without moving the point to a nearby vegetated pixel. Location uncertainty up to 1 km can still exceed the nominal AET pixel size.

Sampling weights correct the deliberate oversampling of small region–season–stage groups. They do not correct iNaturalist observer effort or missing weather and AET data. Weighted percentages are normalized separately within each region using valid records for each variable. The plots therefore describe the eligible observations, with additional complete-case selection for AET. Scatterplots show individual sampled records without weight-dependent point sizes. Pearson correlations use sampling weights and describe the selected complete cases. They do not adjust for season, latitude, or other confounding variables. Observations are not independent absences, unique animals, or confirmed successful reproduction.

Temperature data were retrieved using the Daymet single-pixel service. AET uses the ORNL TESViS service. Region geometry comes from Natural Earth. Source responses, the sampling manifest, and per-observation date windows are cached beside this report.
